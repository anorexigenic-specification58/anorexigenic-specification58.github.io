About

What I do

I work on problems involving forecasting, optimization, commercial analytics, generative AI and machine learning systems.

What I'm interested in

I'm particularly interested in understanding problems deeply, building strong technical foundations and turning difficult problems into systematic, reproducible processes.

Why Thinking Better?

This site is an attempt to make my learning and thinking explicit.

I want to understand not only what works, but why it works, how it fails, and whether the process can be repeated by someone else.
