🔍 What is Semantica?

Semantica is a powerful yet simple tool that helps your AI applications work smarter. Think of it as giving your AI a journal and a rulebook. It lets your AI remember previous conversations and information (context), and it can show you exactly why it made a certain decision (accountability).

This technology is called a "context graph" – a way to link all your data and knowledge together visually, allowing AI to find answers with more confidence and accuracy.

✨ Key Features

🧩 Context Graph Engine

Automatically builds a visual map of your data, helping AI understand relationships between concepts.

🛡️ Accountable AI

Easily see the chain of reasoning behind AI outputs, ensuring transparency and trust.

💾 Persistent Memory

Let AI remember past interactions and learned facts for more real, human-like conversations.

🚀 Graph-RAG Powered

Uses knowledge graphs to find precise answers to complex questions that ordinary AI misses.
